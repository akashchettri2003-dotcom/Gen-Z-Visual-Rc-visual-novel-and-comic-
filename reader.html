The Beginning

Every story begins with a single moment. Sometimes that moment looks ordinary. Sometimes it changes everything.

The sky was unusually quiet that evening. The village had already begun to disappear beneath the shadows of the surrounding mountains.

Nobody noticed the strange light in the distance. Nobody except one person.

Standing at the edge of the old forest, the young traveler watched the light grow brighter with every passing second.

There was something familiar about it. Something that made the traveler feel as if this moment had happened before.

Then the light vanished.

And the world changed.
